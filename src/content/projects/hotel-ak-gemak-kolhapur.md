## Project overview

Hotel AK Gemak in Kolhapur is one of PBJ Infra's completed hospitality pool projects — a mosaic-tiled swimming pool built around a raised garden island, reached by white bridge walkways on either side, with a poolside lounge deck for guest seating. It was completed in 2023 and draws on the same [pool tiling and finishing](/services/swimming-pool-tiles/) and [pool filtration](/services/pool-filtration-systems/) discipline PBJ applies across its aquatic work.

## PBJ's approach

The pool, the raised island at its centre and the bridge walkways connecting it to the deck were designed as one structure rather than an island retrofitted into an existing pool — so the waterline, the island's edge and the bridge supports all sit on the same engineering. The surrounding deck was finished with built-in lounge seating for guest use.

## Construction / execution

Structural shell and waterproofing came first, then filtration and circulation, then the blue mosaic tile finish across the pool and island edges. The bridge walkways, raised island structure and poolside landscaping and seating were completed alongside the pool finish.

## Final result

A completed swimming pool and leisure deck at Hotel AK Gemak, Kolhapur, in active guest use since 2023.
