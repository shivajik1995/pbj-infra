## Project Overview

The Adventure Waterpark is a family entertainment waterpark in Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra — one of PBJ Infra's five signature projects and a strong example of hospitality-scale aquatic construction beyond a conventional residential swimming pool.

The project brings together a multi-lane slide tower, children's aqua-play structure with open slides and fountains, a splash pad, and a large curved leisure pool. Behind these visitor-facing attractions is an integrated aquatic infrastructure comprising filtration, circulation, waterproofing and safety systems designed to support continuous public use.

The project was executed over approximately one year, completed in 2023, and has been operating since.

## Client Requirement

The client wanted to develop a family-oriented waterpark that could provide multiple recreational experiences within a single destination.

The brief required a combination of attractions suitable for children, families and recreational visitors rather than a single swimming or leisure pool. The development therefore needed to accommodate a large activity area, dedicated children's play features, water slides and a larger leisure pool.

The key requirements included:

* A multi-lane water slide attraction
* A dedicated children's aqua-play area
* Open slides and interactive fountains
* A splash-pad experience
* A large leisure pool
* Reliable filtration and water-circulation infrastructure
* Durable pool construction suitable for regular public use
* Safe integration of the different attractions and pool areas

The challenge was to bring these different experiences together into one coordinated aquatic environment while maintaining the structural and operational requirements of a public leisure facility.

## The Challenge

Unlike a residential swimming pool, a waterpark brings several construction disciplines together at the same time.

The pool structures need to retain water reliably, while filtration and circulation systems must support multiple aquatic zones. Large attractions also introduce additional structural and coordination requirements.

For Adventure Waterpark, the slide tower and children's aqua-play structure had to be integrated with the surrounding pools and civil works. Slide run-outs needed to connect correctly with the receiving areas, while the activity zones had to maintain clear circulation for visitors.

Another important consideration was the relationship between the visible attractions and the infrastructure beneath them. Pool construction, waterproofing, drainage, filtration, circulation and equipment connections all needed to be coordinated before the facility could operate as one system.

The project therefore required more than individual pool construction — it required the different structural, aquatic and attraction components to function together.

## PBJ's Approach

PBJ Infra approached the project by treating the waterpark as one integrated aquatic system rather than a collection of separate attractions.

The same structural and filtration discipline used in PBJ's swimming-pool projects was scaled for a larger public leisure environment.

### Aquatic Structures

The leisure and activity pools were developed as the foundation of the visitor experience, with construction focused on structural integrity, waterproofing and long-term aquatic use.

### Attraction Integration

The slide tower, open slides, children's aqua-play structure and fountains were coordinated with the surrounding pool and civil works so that the attractions could operate as part of the overall waterpark.

### Water Systems

Filtration and circulation infrastructure was integrated across the aquatic areas to support the continuous movement and treatment of water required by the facility.

### Safety & Movement

Pool depths, slide run-outs, activity zones and visitor circulation were considered together so that the different attractions could coexist within the same recreational environment.

## Construction / Execution

The project was executed as an integrated aquatic and leisure construction scope over approximately one year.

PBJ Infra's work brought together the major components of the development, including the leisure pool and activity pools, slide-tower foundations and structure, imported slide and aqua-play equipment, filtration systems and supporting safety infrastructure.

The execution followed a coordinated sequence:

**01 — Site**

Preparation and coordination of the site for the planned pools, attractions and supporting infrastructure.

**02 — Structure**

Construction of the pool structures, activity areas and foundations required for the slide and play attractions.

**03 — Systems**

Integration of filtration, circulation, hydraulic connections and supporting aquatic systems.

**04 — Signature**

Completion of finishes, attraction integration and the final details required to turn the construction into a functioning visitor experience.

This integrated approach allowed the structural, aquatic and specialist attraction elements to progress as parts of the same project.

## The Waterpark Experience

The completed waterpark provides multiple experiences within one destination.

The multi-lane slide tower forms one of the major attractions, offering different slide configurations for recreational use.

The children's aqua-play structure creates a dedicated interactive environment with open slides, animal features and fountains, while the splash pad provides an additional family-focused water activity.

A large curved leisure pool completes the main aquatic environment, creating a broader space for relaxation and recreational use.

Together, these elements create a waterpark experience that serves different age groups and different styles of recreation.

## Engineering Behind the Experience

The finished attractions are only one part of a functioning waterpark.

Behind every pool and slide is a network of structural and aquatic systems that must work continuously.

For Adventure Waterpark, PBJ Infra coordinated:

* Pool and structural construction
* Waterproofing
* Filtration systems
* Water circulation
* Hydraulic connections
* Attraction integration
* Slide run-out areas
* Activity-pool construction
* Children's aqua-play installation
* Safety considerations
* Finishing and commissioning

The objective was to ensure that the visitor-facing attractions were supported by the underlying infrastructure required for a continuously operating public facility.
