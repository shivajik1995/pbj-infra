## Project overview

The Adventure Waterpark is a family entertainment waterpark in the Pune–Latur corridor — one of PBJ Infra's five signature projects and its clearest example of hospitality-scale construction beyond a single residential pool. It combines multiple water attractions with the filtration and safety infrastructure a public facility needs, completed in 2023 and operating since.
