## Project overview

The Farm House near Latur is PBJ Infra's documented residential project in the region and the reference behind its [farmhouse construction](/services/farmhouse-construction/) service. Latur is described on PBJ's own site as a major operational hub, and this completed build is the project evidence behind that — with PBJ's Pune team also delivering the [Adventure Waterpark](/projects/adventure-waterpark-ahilyanagar/) in nearby Ahilyanagar.

## The challenge

A farmhouse on rural land is as much a site project as a building project: water has to be found and stored, sewage handled on site, power brought in, and an access road built before construction can run efficiently.

*Content required: the specific site conditions near Latur (water availability, access, soil) need a short note from PBJ's site team.*

## PBJ's approach

PBJ worked the project in the order a remote plot demands: site works and infrastructure first so the rest of the build could proceed without repeated mobilisation, then the residence, then landscaping and external works. Because the same in-house team covers structure, any pool, and the grounds, the phases could overlap. Clients who are not local received regular progress updates through the build.

## Construction / execution

Access, water supply and storage, and drainage were established first. The residence followed, built in a mix of rustic and contemporary materials. External works — approach, boundary, lawn and orchard areas — completed the project.

## Final result

A completed, in-use farmhouse near Latur, built end-to-end by one team with its supporting infrastructure designed for the property rather than bolted on.
