## Project overview

AKAYA Resort sits inland from the beaches of Gokarna, on the Karnataka coast, and is built as a cluster of ferrocement thin-shell dome cottages rather than conventional rooms. Each cottage is a monolithic mortar shell — column-free, curved in every direction, with door and window openings and a tunnel-form entrance shaped into the geometry. PBJ Infra built the shells and the connecting site works, set among an existing coconut and areca grove.

It is the larger companion to PBJ's Rabbit Dome House in Pune — the same [ferrocement dome](/services/ferrocement-dome/) and [ferro concrete design](/services/ferro-concrete-design/) technique, repeated across a whole site and carried out of Maharashtra to the Karnataka coast.

## The challenge

Repeating a thin-shell dome across a resort is harder than building one. Each shell still has to be set out, reinforced and mortar-applied by hand, but now to a consistent standard across many units, on a sloping coastal site with laterite soil and an existing tree canopy to work around. The tunnel-form entrances and the larger openings had to be reinforced and built into each shell from the start, and the units had to be positioned for privacy and drainage without clearing the grove.

## PBJ's approach

PBJ treated the domes as a repeatable structural system. Shell geometry, mesh layering and mortar mix were designed once for the cottage type and then reproduced unit by unit, with the openings and the tunnel entrances set into the formwork rather than cut later. Site works — levelling, edge retaining, paths and drainage — were run alongside the shells so the grove and the contours were kept largely intact.

## Construction / execution

Each dome was built up over formwork and an armature: layered steel mesh, then cement mortar applied and compacted in controlled layers, then curing, then external weather-coating and internal finishing. The cottages were built in sequence while the laterite paths, retaining kerbs and planting were laid in between. The full scope ran over roughly a year.

## Final result

A dome-cottage resort at Gokarna — a set of monolithic ferrocement shells among the palms, each a single curved space with its own tunnel entrance — and PBJ Infra's proof that its dome capability scales from one house to a whole site.
