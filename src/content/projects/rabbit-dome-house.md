## Project overview

The Rabbit Dome House is a ferrocement dome residence in Pune and PBJ Infra's clearest demonstration of specialised structural work — a building where the dome is the living space, not a decorative roof over a conventional frame. It is the built reference behind PBJ's [ferrocement dome](/services/ferrocement-dome/) and [ferro concrete design](/services/ferro-concrete-design/) services.

## PBJ's approach

The dome was treated as a structural-engineering problem first. Shell geometry, the number and spacing of reinforcement-mesh layers, and the mortar mix were designed for the span and loads. Door, window and skylight openings were edge-reinforced and set into the geometry from the start, since cutting large openings into a finished stressed shell is difficult and risky.

## Construction / execution

The shell was built up over formwork and an armature: layered steel mesh, then cement mortar applied and compacted in controlled layers, then careful curing. Internal and external finishing followed, with a weather coat on the exterior and plaster inside.

## Final result

A completed, occupied ferrocement dome house in Pune — a monolithic, column-free structure built with a fraction of the material of an equivalent framed building, and the project PBJ Infra points to when a client asks whether it has actually done this before.
