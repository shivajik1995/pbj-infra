## Project overview

The Natural Lagoon Pool in Chatrapati Sambhaji Nagar is PBJ Infra's built example of its Trusted Lagoon line — a swimming pool designed not to look like one. It is the reference project behind the [lagoon pool construction](/services/lagoon-pool-construction/) service and draws on the same [pool filtration](/services/pool-filtration-systems/) and [pool tiling and finishing](/services/swimming-pool-tiles/) discipline as PBJ's conventional pools.

## The challenge

A lagoon pool has to solve a specific problem: keep the water moving and filtered fully in a free-form shell with no straight runs and, usually, a large shallow beach zone that adds volume and needs its own drainage.

*Content required: the specific site constraints for this project need a short account from PBJ's site team.*

## PBJ's approach

The lagoon form, the beach-entry grading, the edge treatment and the filtration were designed together rather than in sequence, so an irregular shell still turns its water over with no dead corners and the soft edges do not compromise skimming and circulation. Underneath the naturalistic finish, it is a fully engineered pool — structural shell, waterproofing and plant to the same standard as any PBJ pool.

## Construction / execution

Structural shell and waterproofing first, then the filtration and circulation plant, then the naturalistic interior finish and the soft edge and shallow-zone detailing.

## Final result

A completed natural lagoon-style pool in Chatrapati Sambhaji Nagar that reads as a natural water body while performing as a properly filtered swimming pool.
