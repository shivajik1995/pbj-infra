## Project overview

Latur Water Park is a family water park that PBJ Infra is currently building at Latur, in the region where the company's second site team and equipment are based. The scope covers the leisure pools, the attraction structures and the filtration, circulation and safety plant a public water park needs.

The photographs on this page are from the pool-structure stage: the main free-form pool has been excavated, its blinding slab cast, and the raft reinforcement laid out across the full footprint, with a stone retaining edge holding the surrounding ground. The build is running to a roughly nine-month programme.

## The challenge

A water park is several construction disciplines running at once: large free-form pool shells that have to retain water reliably, attraction structures with their own foundations, and a filtration and circulation system sized for public use. On this site the main pool is a single large curved shell, which means the reinforcement, waterproofing and the surrounding retaining works all have to be set out and built to a curve rather than a simple rectangle.

## PBJ's approach

PBJ is building the park on the same pool-engineering discipline it applies to residential and resort pools, scaled up for public use and paired with visitor-safety systems. The pool structure is being built first — slab, raft reinforcement, shell and waterproofing — with the retaining works alongside it, before the filtration plant, the attractions and the surrounding hardscape follow.

## Construction / execution

Stage reached so far: site clearing and excavation of the main pool, the stone retaining edge, the blinding slab, and the raft reinforcement across the pool footprint. Still to come: the pool shell and waterproofing, the filtration and circulation plant, the attraction structures, and the decks, landscaping and safety systems. Programme: roughly nine months.
