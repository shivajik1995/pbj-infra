If you're planning a pool, the honest answer is: **a standard residential swimming pool typically takes 4–8 weeks**, depending on design complexity, size and weather conditions. That range comes directly from how PBJ Infra scopes and confirms timelines during consultation — not a marketing estimate.

Here's what actually happens inside that window.

## Stage 1 — Site

Land survey, excavation and site preparation before any structural work begins. This stage is where ground conditions, access and existing landscaping have the biggest effect on schedule — a straightforward residential plot moves fast; a constrained or sloped site takes longer to prepare safely.

## Stage 2 — Structure

The ferro-concrete shell (or, for specialized builds, a dome or custom form) goes in. This is the stage that determines whether an infinity edge or a glass pool wall will actually hold up structurally for years, not just look right at handover.

## Stage 3 — Systems

Filtration, waterproofing and mechanical systems are installed and pressure-tested. Skipping or rushing pressure-testing here is the single biggest cause of pool problems showing up months after handover — it's a fixed step, not a flexible one, regardless of how tight the schedule is.

## Stage 4 — Signature

Tiling, landscaping and final detailing — the result the client actually sees and the stage most before/after photography focuses on. Ceramic, glass mosaic or natural stone tiling is selected and applied here.

## What extends the 4–8 week range

- **Design complexity** — an infinity edge, glass wall or naturalistic lagoon-style pool involves more structural and hydraulic detailing than a standard rectangular pool.
- **Size** — larger pools mean more excavation, more structural work and more tiling.
- **Weather** — monsoon-season builds in Maharashtra need to plan around rain windows for excavation and finishing stages.
