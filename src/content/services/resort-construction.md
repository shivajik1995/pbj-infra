## Overview

Resort construction brings together most of what PBJ Infra does: buildings, pools, water features, landscaping and the underground infrastructure that makes a remote site habitable. The value of a single contractor across all of it is coordination — the pool plant room, the STP, the water storage and the electrical room are planned together, and the wet-leisure areas are built by the team that builds them every day rather than handed to a separate pool contractor at the end.

This service is for hospitality developers, landowners converting agricultural or scenic land into a destination, and existing resort operators expanding their facilities.

## PBJ's approach

PBJ works a resort as a phased programme. Site infrastructure and access come first so the rest of the site can be worked efficiently; structures follow; pools, lagoons and leisure infrastructure are built alongside; landscaping ties it together at the end. Because PBJ's own team covers pools and landscaping as well as structures, phases can overlap without the interface disputes that slow multi-contractor sites.

## Technical considerations

- **Site infrastructure sizing:** water demand, sewage load and power for a working resort are calculated from key count and facility mix, not guessed.
- **Hospitality durability:** wet areas, kitchens and high-traffic circulation are detailed for years of guest use and easy maintenance.
- **Phased opening:** the programme is usually structured so a first cluster of keys and the main facilities can open while later phases continue.
- **Landscape and water integration:** lagoon pools and water features are engineered for water balance and safety, not treated as decoration.
