## Overview

A farmhouse is a residence on a rural plot, and the plot is half the project. Water has to be found and stored, sewage has to be handled on site, power often has to be brought in or generated, and access roads have to be built before anything else can happen. PBJ Infra builds the house and all of that supporting infrastructure under one contract, which is the practical reason most farmhouse clients want a single team: coordinating a structural contractor, a pool contractor, a borewell contractor and a landscaper across a plot two hours from the city rarely goes to plan.

This service is for families building a weekend or retirement home on agricultural land, and for landowners developing a farmhouse for rental or events use.

## PBJ's approach

PBJ starts with the site — access, water, drainage — then builds outward: infrastructure, then the main residence and any guest or staff structures, then the pool and landscaping. Because the same team covers the pool and the grounds, the sequence can be compressed. Clients who live elsewhere get regular progress updates so the distance does not mean losing sight of the build.

## Technical considerations

- **Water security:** borewell yield, rainwater harvesting and storage capacity are planned against realistic household and irrigation demand.
- **On-site sewage:** a septic or packaged treatment system sized correctly for the number of occupants, kept clear of the water source.
- **Materials for the setting:** exposed stone, brick, timber and lime finishes are common farmhouse choices; PBJ builds in these as readily as in conventional RCC and blockwork.
- **Security and low-maintenance detailing:** for a house that sits empty part of the week.
