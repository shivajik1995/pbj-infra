## Overview

Swimming pool construction is PBJ Infra's most established service line — structural shell, filtration system and tile finish are designed and built by the same in-house team rather than split across subcontractors, which is what makes an infinity edge or a glass pool wall actually hold up under real hydraulic pressure over years, not just look right on handover day.

This service is for homeowners building a residential pool, farmhouse or resort owners adding a pool as part of a larger leisure development, and anyone who needs the pool's structure, filtration and finish coordinated as one build rather than three separate contracts.
