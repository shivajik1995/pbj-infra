## Overview

Swimming pool construction is PBJ Infra's most established service line — structural shell, filtration system and tile finish are designed and built by the same in-house team rather than split across subcontractors, which is what makes an infinity edge or a glass pool wall actually hold up under real hydraulic pressure over years, not just look right on handover day.

This service is for homeowners building a residential pool, farmhouse or resort owners adding a pool as part of a larger leisure development, and anyone who needs the pool's structure, filtration and finish coordinated as one build rather than three separate contracts.

## PBJ's approach

The shell, the filtration plant and the tile finish are designed together from the first 3D visualisation, not handed off between separate contractors as the build progresses. An infinity edge is engineered for the structural and hydraulic detailing a vanishing edge requires before the shell is poured; the filtration layout is planned around the pool's actual turnover needs rather than added afterwards; and the tile setting-out is fixed in the plan so mosaic lines follow steps, benches and curved edges cleanly. Every build carries PBJ's 5-year structural and 2-year equipment warranty.

## Technical considerations

- **Vanishing edges:** an infinity pool's edge depends on a correctly engineered catch basin and balance tank, sized to the overflow it needs to handle — not just a lowered wall.
- **Turnover and filtration:** pump and filter sizing follow the pool's volume and expected use, the same turnover discipline PBJ applies on its [pool filtration systems](/services/pool-filtration-systems/) work.
- **Tile and finish:** a fully tiled shell holds colour and lasts longer than a waterline band over plaster — see [pool tiling and finishing](/services/swimming-pool-tiles/) for the full trade-off.
- **Glass-wall and indoor pools:** glass panel walls and indoor installations add structural and waterproofing considerations a standard outdoor shell doesn't need.
