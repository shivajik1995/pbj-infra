Pune is PBJ Infra's home base — the address on file, the team's daily starting point, and the place most of the company's documented project history is concentrated. Panchshil Realty, Nyati Group, Kolte Patil Developers, Godrej Properties and D.Y. Patil are among the developers whose projects PBJ Infra has worked on, all with a strong presence in and around Pune.

## What PBJ builds in Pune

[Swimming pool construction](/services/swimming-pool-construction/) is the most established Pune-area service, covering infinity, glass-wall and indoor pools, [filtration systems](/services/pool-filtration-systems/), [tiling and finishing](/services/swimming-pool-tiles/) and [lagoon-style pools](/services/lagoon-pool-construction/). PBJ's specialised [ferro-concrete design](/services/ferro-concrete-design/) and [ferrocement dome](/services/ferrocement-dome/) work runs out of the Pune team's technical capability, as does [land development](/services/land-development/) for greenfield sites.

## Verified Pune project history

The [Rabbit Dome House](/projects/rabbit-dome-house/) — a completed ferrocement dome residence in Pune — is PBJ Infra's built reference for specialised thin-shell work. A Luxury Infinity Pool is also attributed to a Pune residence on PBJ's portfolio, though the source site is inconsistent on whether it is Pune or Mumbai, so its case study is held back until PBJ confirms the location. See [/projects/](/projects/) for what is published.

## How Pune projects are run

The founder, core supervisors and equipment are based in Phursungi. Permits and regulatory compliance for Pune-region projects are handled directly by PBJ, and every build carries a 5-year structural warranty and a 2-year equipment warranty.
