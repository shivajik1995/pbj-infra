Pune is PBJ Infra's home base — the address on file, the team's daily starting point, and the place most of the company's documented project history is concentrated. Panchshil Realty, Nyati Group, Kolte Patil Developers and D.Y. Patil are among the developers PBJ Infra has worked with, all headquartered in or around Pune.

## What PBJ builds in Pune

Swimming pool construction is the most established Pune-area service, covering infinity, glass-wall and indoor pools, filtration systems and tiling. PBJ's specialized ferro-concrete and ferrocement dome work is also based out of the Pune team's technical capability.

## Verified Pune project history

Two signature projects on PBJ's own portfolio page are attributed to Pune: a Luxury Infinity Pool at a Pune residence, and the Rabbit Dome House, a ferrocement dome structure. Full case-study pages for both are a content item in progress — see [/projects/](/projects/) for what's published so far.
