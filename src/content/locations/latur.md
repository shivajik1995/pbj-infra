Latur is where PBJ Infra's work extends beyond its home region. The company is headquartered in Phursungi, Pune, but its own site describes Latur as a major operational hub — and unlike the wider list of cities PBJ can serve, Latur is backed by real, completed project evidence.

## Verified Latur project history

Two of PBJ Infra's five signature projects are attributed to Latur:

- The [Adventure Waterpark](/projects/adventure-waterpark-latur/) in the Pune–Latur corridor — a family entertainment waterpark completed in 2023 and operating since, and PBJ's clearest example of hospitality-scale construction.
- A completed [Farm House](/projects/farm-house-latur/) — a custom rural residence built end-to-end with its own site infrastructure.

## What PBJ builds in Latur

The services with Latur evidence or a natural fit for the region's land are [swimming pool construction](/services/swimming-pool-construction/), [waterpark construction](/services/waterpark-construction/), [farmhouse construction](/services/farmhouse-construction/) and [land development](/services/land-development/) for greenfield and agricultural plots. PBJ's specialised [ferrocement dome](/services/ferrocement-dome/) and [ferro concrete design](/services/ferro-concrete-design/) capability is available to Latur projects from the Pune technical team.

## How Latur projects are run

Latur sits on the Pune–Latur corridor, so PBJ's Pune-based site team, supervisors and equipment reach the region directly. Permits, approvals and compliance with local building authorities are handled by PBJ rather than routed through a local intermediary, and every build carries the same 5-year structural and 2-year equipment warranty as PBJ's Pune work.
